Apartment Model

Your apartment as a low-poly model, to scale from the Oct 18 scan. Your own pieces are rebuilt from the scan at their measured sizes. The TV wall is the top edge of the living room; the balcony is on its right.

Selected

Tap a piece to edit it. Names ending in (?) are my guesses from the scan, so rename or remove what I got wrong.
Tap a wall to move it, drag its round ends to stretch it, or tap a room's floor to rename or resize it. Walls snap to each other, so a doubled wall can be dragged onto its twin and removed.
