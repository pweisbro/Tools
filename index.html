Tools

Small single-page tools. Each one runs in the browser; the Wi-Fi Analyzer also needs a small script on your computer.

  • Wi-Fi Analyzer

    Signal strength and which 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz channels are crowded, from a scan run on your own computer.

    ›

  • Options Watch

    Live stock and options dashboard for any ticker. Opens in Claude and only loads when I'm signed in.

    ↗

  • Options Replay

    Replay a past trading day minute by minute and try option plays with paper money, by hand or with an agent. Simulation only.

    ›

  • Laundry Timer

    Scan the sign on a washer or dryer to start a timer on your iPhone. Includes a QR and NFC sign maker.

    ›

  • Apartment Model

    A to-scale, low-poly model of the apartment built from the scan. Drag furniture around in plan, 3D or walk-through view and save layouts.
