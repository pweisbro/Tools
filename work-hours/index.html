Work Hours Log

Totals paid time from the punches your Shortcuts write to work-log.csv. The file is read on your device. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

Log format

One line per punch, with the columns date,time,event,source.

2026-11-05,07:58,IN,auto
2026-11-05,12:03,OUT,auto
2026-11-05,12:47,IN,auto
2026-11-05,16:41,OUT,auto
2026-11-05,16:45,CONFIRM,manual

Paid time is each IN to the next OUT. The gaps between them are unpaid breaks, and a gap over an hour is flagged. A gap of 5 minutes or less is the phone bouncing on the edge of the radius, so it counts as paid and is not shown as a break. CONFIRM marks a day you have checked. Lines from the Lunch shortcut use the source manual and show with an asterisk.
